In 2026 Dave Hamilton published *Weird Guide Britain: An Atlas of 300 Curious Corners, Wild Wonders and Sublime Structures*. It collects follies, rock formations, abandoned ruins, film locations, wartime defences, and plain oddities from all over Britain, each with coordinates and directions.

This site is my record of actually going to them. Each page is my visit to the weird thing: when I went, who came along, what it was like, and the photos I took. The map on the front page fills in as I go.

The book is the guide and the inspiration. If a place here catches your eye, [buy the book](https://wildthingspublishing.com/product/weird-guide-britain/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} for the proper write-up, history, and directions. It’s a wonderful gem for anyone looking to explore more in the United Kingdom.
