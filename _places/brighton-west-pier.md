Emma and I had a weekend to ourselves while Jillian visited California to support her parents. We did a day trip to Brighton to enjoy a little chilly sunshine and the beach. Little did we know, we were in the presence of a weird thing: the West Pier.

Jillian and I had visited Brighton in 2023 and noticed the metal frame of the old structure. It has a mystical quality to it. I imagine it looks very spooky on a cloudy and misty morning.

The pier opened in 1866 to a design by Eugenius Birch, who built seaside piers all over Victorian England. It closed in 1975 and never reopened. Fires gutted what was left of it in 2003. Storms have been pulling it apart ever since, which is how it ended up as a skeleton standing out in the water.

While Emma and I didn't truly appreciate its uniqueness, we did visit an upside down house and get tasty ice cream. Brighton's a town we love to visit (it's a little over an hour from London by train). It's a place I think a lot about retiring in. I'll get better pictures next time I'm back!
