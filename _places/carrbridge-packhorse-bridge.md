In 2024, my family and I visited a different country every month of the year, 10 of them in Europe. November was Scotland. We made Aberdeen our homebase for a long weekend. We joined a bus tour into the Highlands where we got to take in a lot of sights, including this unique bridge.

The Carrbridge packhorse bridge is known as the oldest stone bridge in the Highlands, and for the nickname that comes with it: the coffin bridge. We learned that villagers would carry their dead over it. It was built in 1717 so that funeral parties could still reach the churchyard at Duthil when the River Dulnain was too high to cross. It looks as fragile as it does because the Muckle Spate flood of 1829 tore its parapets away and left the bare arch standing.

I thought it was beautiful in the snow. I think we got some great pictures.

The more I look through my Weird Guide, the more places I'm finding that I've visited. Just before this stop, we got to meet some Hairy Cows! They were super chill.
