{%- comment -%} Every place in the order the visits happened, oldest first. Sets `ordered`. `visited` is only a date, so two places seen on the same day tie, and Liquid's sort is not stable: the tie fell back to filename order. Within one day the `order` field settles it. A day with a single visit does not need the field. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ordered = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign days = site.places | group_by_exp: "p", "p.visited | date: '%Y%m%d'" | sort: "name" -%} {%- for day in days -%} {%- assign within = day.items | sort: "order" -%} {%- assign ordered = ordered | concat: within -%} {%- endfor -%}
