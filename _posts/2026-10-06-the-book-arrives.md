The Weird Guide arrived in the post on 6 October. It was an early birthday present from my family, and I can say with confidence that it was the right gift, because I asked for it. That is how we do things these days.

Until now, every Weird 300 on this site was an accident. We stumbled onto Fingal's Cave on a boat trip, the Orbit from a playground, and the Pairc Deer Raiders Memorial on the way to a concert, and only found out later that all three were in the book.

From here on, it is on purpose. This is the official start of working my way through Dave Hamilton's guide, fully aware of what is in it. Three were found by luck. The rest will be found on purpose.
