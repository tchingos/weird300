The Weird Guide arrived in the post on 6 October 2026. It was an early birthday present from my family, and I can say with confidence that it was the right gift, because I asked for it.

Until now, the Weird 300 places I stumbled upon were by accident. I came upon [Fingal’s Cave]({{ '/places/fingals-cave/' | relative_url }}) on a boat trip, the [ArcelorMittal Orbit]({{ '/places/orbit/' | relative_url }}) from a playground, and the [Pairc Deer Raiders Memorial]({{ '/places/pairc-deer-raiders-memorial/' | relative_url }}) on the way to a concert.

Well, now it’s on purpose. In 2027 (and beyond), I’m looking to spend more time outside and see more of my beautiful adopted home country. I’ve never been to Wales, and I’ve barely scratched the surface of the beauty in England and Scotland. My plan is to use this as an excuse to get me and my family outside, even if it’s just to take a photo of something weird.
