In October, Jillian, Emma, and I spent an afternoon at Chiswick House having our photo taken for this year’s Christmas card. We worked with the same photographer we used last year and she was wonderful. We spent over an hour touring the gardens and admiring the exterior of the house while having our photo taken. The weather was kind to us to boot!

Now for some fun Chiswick House facts: The house is a Palladian villa in West London, finished in 1729 for Richard Boyle, the 3rd Earl of Burlington. Burlington and William Kent then laid out the gardens with winding paths and loose clumps of trees, at a time when every other grand garden in the country was straight lines and clipped symmetry. It's often called the first garden of its kind in England. It made for a lovely backdrop.

We came home with hundreds of photos to pick through along with a clear winner for our Christmas card. The card has been designed and will be sent out a bit early this year.
