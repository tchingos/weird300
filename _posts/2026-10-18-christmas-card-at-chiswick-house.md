In October, Jillian, Emma, and I spent a day at Chiswick House having our photo taken for this year's Christmas card. The weather was kind to us, which you can't count on in London in October.

The house is a Palladian villa in west London, finished in 1729 for Richard Boyle, the 3rd Earl of Burlington. Its gardens were laid out with William Kent, and are usually counted among the beginnings of the English landscape garden. It made for a very good backdrop.

We came home with hundreds of photos to pick through, including some really cute ones. One has already ended up on the [about page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}). The rest are for the card, which goes out in December.
